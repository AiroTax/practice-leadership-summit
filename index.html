Where exactly is it?

A hotel within an hour of Clifton, NJ, confirmed by Nov 12. Free parking is part of our venue terms. A small room block is available for Friday and Saturday nights for teams driving 45 minutes or more.

How do the CE hours work?

Up to 12 AGD PACE hours under subject code 550, through a PACE-approved co-sponsor. NJ dentists need 40 hours per two-year cycle and can apply practice-management hours to renewal. Every attendee receives a certificate. Hygienists and assistants: we are confirming how NJ counts these hours for your licence and will tell you before the event.

Who should we bring?

The owner plus the two people who will run the plan with you, often your office manager and a clinical lead. Teams of three get the best price and qualify for the full-team WinDSO bonus.

Who is teaching?

Omar Awad, Harvard Kennedy School negotiation faculty, leads Negotiating change with your team. Kasper facilitators run every session, with one coach for every six tables. Two keynote speakers will be announced in late November.

What if it snows?

If a storm makes travel unsafe, we decide by Thursday Jan 7 and move the summit to Jan 23–24 at the same venue. Your registration moves with it, or you can take a full refund.

Cancellations and substitutions

Full refund until Dec 4, 50% until Dec 18, then a credit toward a future summit. You can swap a team member’s name at any time at no cost.

Will anyone try to sell to us?

Not in the sessions. CE rules keep the educational program free of product promotion. Partners have a separate Solutions Lounge you can visit during breaks if you want to.

What is the WinDSO bonus?

WinDSO Empower & Grow 2027 tickets (Mar 3–5, Las Vegas; travel not included) go to qualifying practices while they last: 15 for early-bird teams of three, 20 for full teams after Dec 4, and 10 for referrals.
